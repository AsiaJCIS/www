## AsiaJCIS 2027

### Conference Venue

**Juntendo University, Urayasu-Hinode Campus**, Chiba, Japan.

6-8-1 Hinode, Urayasu, Chiba 279-0013, Japan
（〒279-0013 千葉県浦安市日の出6-8-1）

### Access

- **From Haneda Airport (HND):** about 40 minutes.
- **From Narita Airport (NRT):** about 60 minutes.
- **From Tokyo Station:** about 20 minutes by train to Shin-Urayasu Station, then about 10 minutes by bus.
- Tokyo Disney Resort is located at the neighboring station, and can be a convenient transfer point.
- A **shuttle bus** for conference participants is being planned.

Haneda Airport is recommended for international arrivals. Detailed directions and the shuttle schedule will be announced closer to the conference.

### Explore the Area

The campus sits right on the **Tokyo Bay waterfront**, and central Tokyo is only about 20 minutes away — so it is easy to combine the conference with some sightseeing.

- **Tokyo Disney Resort** is at the neighboring station (Maihama). Arriving a day early or staying on afterwards is a popular option.
- **Central Tokyo** is within easy reach: Tokyo Station (~20 min), and from there Asakusa (Sensō-ji temple), Ginza, Akihabara, and more.
- **Tokyo Bay** offers waterfront walks and views, with large bayside parks nearby.

**Plan your visit:** [Things to do in Urayasu (TripAdvisor)](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g298162-Activities-Urayasu_Chiba_Prefecture_Kanto.html) · [Urayasu tourism (official)](https://www.urayasu-kankou.jp/) · [Shin-Urayasu area guide (Japanese)](https://urayasu-sanpo.com/11/shin-urayasu.html)

### Food

The bay area is known for **fresh seafood and sushi**, and Japan's casual *izakaya* pubs are a great way to unwind after sessions. A conference **banquet featuring local seafood** is planned — see the Social Program below.

### Good to Know

- **Season:** the conference is in early August. Tokyo summers are warm and humid, so light clothing is recommended. It is also the season of summer festivals (*matsuri*) and fireworks.
- **Getting around:** trains are frequent and signposted in English; a rechargeable IC card (Suica / PASMO / Welcome Suica) makes travel easy.
- **Hotels:** there are many hotels around Maihama and Shin-Urayasu, from business hotels to bayside resorts (see the [Accommodation](accommodation.md) page).
