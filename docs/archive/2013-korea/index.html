Overview

The 8th Asia Joint Conference on Information Security (AsiaJCIS 2013) will be held in Seoul, Korea on July 25 through 26, 2013. The focus of this conference is on the technical and practical aspects of the security applications. The conference will serve as a forum to present new results from the academic research community as well as from the industry. Original papers are solicited for submission to AsiaJCIS. In addition, several distinguished security experts will be invited to give technical talks.
The proceedings of AsiaJCIS will be published by the Conference Publishing Services (CPS). Your publication will be submitted to the IEEE digital library : IEEE Xplore.

Instructions for Authors

Authors are invited to submit original papers: they must not substantially duplicate work that any of the authors have published elsewhere or have submitted in parallel to any other conferences that have proceedings. The submission must be anonymous, with no author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, or obvious references. The paper submission guidelines can be found on the conference website.
