August 20-21, Taipei, Taiwan

THE GRAND HOTEL TAIPEI

Overview

The 15th Asia Joint Conference on Information Security (AsiaJCIS 2020) will be held in Taipei, Taiwan on August 20 through 21, 2020. The focus of this conference is on the technical and practical aspects of the security applications. The conference will serve as a forum to present new results from the academic research community as well as from the industry. Original papers are solicited for submission to AsiaJCIS. In addition, several distinguished security experts will be invited to give technical talks.

Distinguished accepted and presented papers in AsiaJCIS 2020, after further revisions, will be published in the special issues of the following international journals:

Journal of Internet Technology (SCIE) Mathematics - MDPI (SCIE) Sensors - MDPI (SCIE)
