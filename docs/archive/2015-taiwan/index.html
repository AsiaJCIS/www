Overview

The 10th Asia Joint Conference on Information Security (AsiaJCIS 2015) will be held in Kaohsiung, Taiwan on May 24 through 26, 2015. The focus of this conference is on the technical and practical aspects of the security applications. The conference will serve as a forum to present new results from the academic research community as well as from the industry. The proceedings of AsiaJCIS 2015 will be published by IEEE CPS (Conference Publishing Services).
