Location of Hanyang University
  • From President Hotel
    City Hall station : Take the subway line number 2(Green Line) at the City Hall station toward Euljiro 1(il)-ga, Wangsimni or Jamsil, and take off at HanYang University. It takes about 14min, 8 stations to go.

    Euljiro 1(il)-ga (or often it says euljiro ib gu station) : Take the subway line number 2(Green Line) at the Euljiro 1(il)-ga station toward Euljiro 3(sam)-ga, Wangsimni or Jamsil, and take off at HanYang University. It takes about 12min, 7 stations to go.


  • From Incheon International Airport
     The Incheon International Airport (IIA), in the city of Incheon, is the biggest and main international airport in Korea. It takes about 60-minute car ride to HanYang University from there. It costs about U$40 for a regular taxi and U$60 for deluxe (black) taxi. For more information on transportation from the airport to downtown Seoul, please visit the IIA website at http://www.airport.or.kr/eng/airport. All fares are payable in Korean Won only. Taxi fares from InIncheon International Airport to HanYang University are quite expensive. Since Seoul subways are not directly connected to the Incheon Airport yet, we recommend taking the Limousine Bus from the airport and transferring to a taxi at a bus stop near the University. Please refer to the following list of possible transportation routes to HanYang University.

    Limousine Bus
    To get to the University, you can take the DoBong and GahngBook bound buses, which can be found at the 3B10A Bus Stops at the Incheon Airport Entrance. The bus fare is W13,000. Buses operate at 20-40 minute intervals from 6:00 am until 10:00 pm. You should get off at the SungDong GuChung stop. Then take Bus 2014 (Green), 2013 (Green), 410 (Blue), or 263 (Blue). If you have any questions, you can call the Korea City Air Terminal at +82-2-551-0790.


    Taxi
    Most international airline companies offer flights to Incheon International Airport, which was opened on April 1, 2001. From the airport, it costs approximately KR W50,000-60,000 by taxi, which can carry up to 4 people, to HanYang University. The fare of a regular taxi starts from W1,900 while a deluxe (black) taxi starts from KR W3,600. Most taxi drivers know the direction to HanYang University (pronounced as 'HahnYahng DaeHakGyo').

    Subway
    Take AREX(Airport Railroad EXpress). It costs KRW3100 and takes about 33min from Incheon International Airport to Gimpo International Airport. Transfer to the subway line number 5(Purple Line) at Gimpo International Airport toward Wangsimni, Sangil-dong or Macheon. Transfer to the line number 2(Green Line) at the Wangsimni station (takes 53min from Gimpo) toward Seongsu or Jamsil, and take off at HanYang University which is just one station away from Wangsimni station.


    Airport Pick-up Service
    HanYang offers an airport pick-up service to the University. The fee for this door-to-door service is US $50. If you have any difficulty finding HanYang University, you can call the Office of International Cooperation at +82-2-2220-0045 for assistance.

  • From Gimport International Airport

    Taxi
    From the airport, it costs approximately KR W20,000-30,000 by taxi (KRW30,000-40,000 if take deluxe(black) taxi), which can carry up to 4 people, to HanYang University. The fare of a regular taxi starts from W1,900 while a deluxe (black) taxi starts from KR W3,600. Most taxi drivers know the direction to HanYang University (pronounced as 'HahnYahng DaeHakGyo').

    Subway
    Take the subway line number 5 at Gimpo International Airport toward Wangsimni, Sangil-dong or Macheon. Transfer to the line number 2 at the Wangsimni station (takes 53min from Gimpo) toward Seongsu or Jamsil, and take off at HanYang University which is just one station away from Wangsimni station.

Location of Hanyang Institute of Technology(HIT)
  • Subway
    Take off at the Hanyang University station line no.2. Follow to the exit no.2 and go up the stairs. Turn right and go straight until you see AmphiTheater. Turn left and go up to the hill and there's no further way to go straight. Turn right, and you will see HIT building on the leftside. There will be follow signs on the day of conference from subway to HIT building.

  • Taxi
    Tell the driver to go "HIT building in HanYang University". Directions for HIT building : pass the Hanyang University Hospital and go straight about 200 meters. It is the first building show up at the leftside. (It is covered with glass.)
