Hotel President

Location

The Hotel President is located in the heart of Seoul, outside the Seoul Public Square. There is a free shuttle bus that operates twice daily (exlucing Sundays and public holidays) from Incheon International Airport to the hotel. There is also a convenient public transport system including limousine bus and subway. For more information, please refer to the website of the HOTEL PRESIDENT
