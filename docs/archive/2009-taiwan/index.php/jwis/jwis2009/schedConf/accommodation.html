Accommodation

Shuttle Bus

Ambassador Hotel

The Ambassador Hotel Kaohsiung is located at the Love River bank, overlooking the enchanting Shoushan, and close to the beautiful Mingsheng Road boulevard, where you can experience the beauty of Kaohsiungfrom any angle. Located in a central traffic area, thus making it easy to get around whether on business or pleasure; the hotel was under reconstruction for 3 years, and now represents the combined features of gallant classic and modern vogue.

 

To make a reservation, please download the "reservation" from, fill it out, and then email to resv@mskh.ambhotel.com.tw

 

  •      The Ambassador Hotel
      If you take a taxi from Kaohsiung International Airport or Taiwan High Speed Rail to Ambassador Hotel, it will take about twenty minutes and NT$250 dollars.
