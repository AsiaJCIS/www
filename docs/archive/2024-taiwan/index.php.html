  AsiaJCIS 2024   

The Asia Joint Conference on Information Security (AsiaJCIS) is one of the crucial international academic exchange events in the field of information security in the Asian region. This conference aims to invite scholars, professionals from academia and industry, researchers from public and private research institutions, as well as experts in information security and cryptography from the world. The conference includes paper presentations and keynote speeches covering topics such as cloud security, system security, data hiding, digital forensics, network security, e-commerce security, database security, communication security, network management, auditing, intrusion detection, cryptography, and other related fields. Not only it facilitates academic exchange, but it also fosters further collaboration among the academic community, government, and industries in the Asian region.
