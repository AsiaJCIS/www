Author instructions

Authors are invited to submit (1) original papers, (2) survey papers. The original papers must not substantially duplicate work that any of the authors have published elsewhere or have submitted in parallel to any other conferences that have proceedings.
 
The paper should be prepared according to the "Authors Instruction for LNCS" (http://www.springer.de/comp/lncs/authors.html) and limited to at most 15 pages in total.

The paper should be submitted electronically, in Portable Document Format (PDF), via the EasyChair website: https://www.easychair.org/conferences/?conf=jwis2011. Authors who have no EasyChair account need to sign up an account.

One of the authors of accepted papers must give oral presentations at the workshop.
