The 6th Joint Workshop on Information Security

Date: October 5-6, 2011
Location: Ambassador Hotel Kaohsiung, 202 Min Sheng 2nd RD., Kaohsiung.

The 6th Joint Workshop on Information Security (JWIS 2011) will be held in kaohsiung, Taiwan on October 5 through 6, 2011. The focus of this workshop is on the technical and practical aspects of the security applications. The workshop will serve as a forum to present new results from the academic research community as well as from the industry. Original papers are solicited for submission to JWIS. In addition, several distinguished security experts will be invited to give technical talks.
 
This workshop was scheduled to be held in Tokai University Takanawa campus in Tokyo on August 4 and August 5, 2011. However, due to the Great Eastern Japan Earthquake on March 11, 2011, the workshop will be relocated to Kaohsiung. We apologize for the inconvenience this may cause to you.
