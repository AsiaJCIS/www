    Overview
  • The 12th Asia Joint Conference on Information Security (AsiaJCIS 2017) will be held in Seoul, Korea on August 10 through 11, 2017. The focus of this conference is on the technical and practical aspects of the security applications. The conference will serve as a forum to present new results from the academic research community as well as from the industry. Original papers are solicited for submission to AsiaJCIS. In addition, several distinguished security experts will be invited to give technical talks. All accepted papers will be submitted to the IEEE Xplore Digital Library, and submitted for indexed by Engineering Index(EI).
    Conference date
  • August 10-11, 2017

    • Instructions for Authors
  • Authors are invited to submit original papers: they must not substantially duplicate work that any of the authors have published elsewhere or have submitted in parallel to any other conferences that have proceedings. The submission must be anonymous, with no author names, affiliations, acknowledgments, or obvious references. Authors of accepted papers must give oral presentation at the conference. The paper submission should conform to the following guidelines: The official language is English. Papers should be limited to 8 pages in length. Extra payment is changed for more pages in full paper case. Formatted to US Letter size (8.5 x 11) pages including figures, tables and references using the IEEE format for conference proceedings (print area of 6-1/2 inches (16.51 cm) wide by 8-7/8 inches (22.51 cm) high, two-column format with columns 3-1/16 inches (7.85 cm) wide with a 3/8 inch (0.81 cm) space between them, single-spaced 10- point Times fully justified text). Please download the IEEE Template in Word version or Latex version. Submissions not conforming to these guidelines may be returned without review. Authors should submit the manuscript in PDF format and make sure that the file will print on a printer that uses letter size (8.5 x 11) paper.
