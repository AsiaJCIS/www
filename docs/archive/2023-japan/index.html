August 15-16, Tokyo, Japan and Virtual

National Institute of Information and Communications Technology

Overview

The 18th Asia Joint Conference on Information Security (AsiaJCIS 2023) will be held in Tokyo, Japan on August 15 through 16, 2023. A web conferencing platform is also provided for virtual attendance. The focus of this conference is on the technical and practical aspects of the security applications. The conference will serve as a forum to present new results from the academic research community as well as from the industry. Original papers are solicited for submission to AsiaJCIS. In addition, several distinguished security experts will be invited to give technical talks.
