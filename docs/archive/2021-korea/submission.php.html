    Instructions for Authors
  • Authors are invited to submit original papers. The papers must not substantially duplicate work that any of the authors have published elsewhere or have submitted in parallel to any other conferences that have proceedings.
  • Double-blind peer review is adopted in this conference. The submission manuscripts must be anonymous, with no author names, affiliations, acknowledgments, or obvious references.
  • Authors of accepted papers MUST give oral presentation at the conference. Papers of authors who will not attend the conference will be deleted from the conference proceedings, and will be announced as no-show paper on the conference site.
