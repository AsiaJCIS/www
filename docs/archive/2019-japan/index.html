August 1-2, Kobe, Japan

Integrated Research Center of Kobe University

Overview

The 14th Asia Joint Conference on Information Security (AsiaJCIS 2019) will be held in Kobe, Japan on August 1 through 2, 2019. The focus of this conference is on the technical and practical aspects of the security applications. The conference will serve as a forum to present new results from the academic research community as well as from the industry. Original papers are solicited for submission to AsiaJCIS. In addition, several distinguished security experts will be invited to give technical talks.
