1. Registration
Download Form and provide the payment for oversea registration (WORD) or Mainland of China (WORD)
The conference registration fee covers the conference proceedings, access to all sessions, meals during the conference (not including any accommodation charge).
We provide general participant registration rate and full-time student registration rate. At least one author of each accepted paper MUST register and MUST meet the deadline of July 20, 2014 for both registration payment and revised paper submission (according to the review comments), so as to ensure that the paper is included in AsiaJCIS 2014 proceedings. If the registration fee is not received by this date and if the revised paper (plus a response letter describing the revisions made) is not submitted by this date, the paper will not be included in AsiaJCIS 2014 proceedings.

2. Contact info.

No refunds will be allowed.

4. Invitation Letter and Visa Information
The conference will email an official invitation letter to each registered author upon a request. If you need the invitation letter, please email us your request as well as your affiliation, postal address and fax number to asiajcis2014@gmail.com and nancy78733@126.com (Mrs. Shen). As soon as you receive the invitation letter, please apply for visa at the Chinese Embassy/Consulate/Visa Office in your country.
