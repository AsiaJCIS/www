Overview

The 9th Asia Joint Conference on Information Security (AsiaJCIS 2014) will be held in Wuhan, China on September 3-5 , 2014. The focus of this conference is on the theoretic, technical and practical aspects of the security applications. The conference will serve as a forum to present new results from the academic research community as well as from the industry. Original papers are solicited for submission to AsiaJCIS. In addition, several distinguished security experts will be invited to give technical talks. All accepted papers will be submitted to the IEEE digital library: IEEE Xplore, and submitted for indexed by Engineering Index (EI).
