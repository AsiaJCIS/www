Accommodation Information

The nearest stations to Okubo Campus of Waseda University are the following stations. (JR EAST Railway Lines in Greater Tokyo)

  • Takadanobaba (Yamanote Line (JR), Tozai Line (Tokyo Metro))
  • Shin-Okubo (Yamanote Line (JR))

So hotels around Shinjuku area and Ikebukuro area are convenient to go to these stations. You can find several hotels at the following web site.

  • Japan Hotels Online

If you have any questions, please contact to icss-jwis2007info@mail.ieice.org.
Shinjuku Area

There are direct bus routes from Narita Airport/Haneda Airport to the following hotels. (Airport Limousine Bus)

  • Shinjuku Washington Hotel
  • Hilton Tokyo
  • Keio Plaza Hotel Tokyo
  • Century Hyatt Tokyo

The following hotel is located at the Seibu Shinjuku Station. It takes five minutes by walk from JR Shinjuku Station. You can use Seibu Shinjuku Line to go to Takadanobaba Station.

  • Shinjuku Prince Hotel
Ikebukuro Area

There are direct bus routes from Narita Airport/Haneda Airport to the following hotels. (Airport Limousine Bus)

  • Crowne Plaza Metropolitan Tokyo
  • Sunshine City Prince Hotel
