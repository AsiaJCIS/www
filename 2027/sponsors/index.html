Call for Sponsors

AsiaJCIS 2027

AsiaJCIS 2027 welcomes sponsorship from industry and academic organizations. Sponsoring the conference is an excellent opportunity to support the information security research community across Asia and to raise your organization's visibility among researchers and practitioners from Japan, Korea, Taiwan, China, and beyond.

Why Sponsor

  • Connect with security researchers and practitioners from academia, industry, and government.
  • Display your banner at the venue and place brochures or flyers for participants.
  • Show your logo on the conference website and on-site.
  • Support students and the next generation of security researchers.

Sponsorship Levels

The planned tiers are shown below. Payment is accepted in Japanese yen (JPY) or US dollars (USD).

Level JPY USD (approx.) Benefits
Platinum ¥200,000 $1,400 Large banner (prime placement) at the venue; brochure/flyer placed at the venue; logo on the website and on-site; acknowledgment in the opening/closing sessions
Gold ¥100,000 $700 Medium banner at the venue; brochure/flyer placed at the venue; logo on the website and on-site
Silver ¥50,000 $350 Small banner at the venue; logo on the website and on-site

Banner size and prominence scale with the sponsorship level. Sponsors may also place brochures or flyers in the venue for participants. Banner specifications (dimensions and format) will be provided to sponsors in advance.

Additional Options (by arrangement)

The following are available on request, subject to availability and the venue layout. If any of these are of interest, please contact us early to discuss.

  • Exhibition booth or demonstration at the venue.
  • Coffee/tea break naming — for example, "Refreshments sponsored by …" displayed at the refreshment area (a limited number of breaks).

Amounts are tentative and may be adjusted. USD figures are approximate equivalents shown for convenience; the exact USD amount will be confirmed closer to the conference.

Individual Supporters

Support is not limited to organizations — individuals are also warmly welcome. Individual contributions start from just ¥1,000 (or the equivalent in USD). With your consent, your name will be listed as a supporter on the conference website. This is a simple way for members of the community to help sustain AsiaJCIS.

How to Become a Sponsor or Supporter

Organizations and individuals interested in supporting AsiaJCIS 2027 are invited to get in touch. Please see the Contact page; a dedicated sponsorship contact and payment details (bank transfer, etc.) will be announced shortly.

All sponsorships are subject to acceptance by the organizers. Please also see our Code of Conduct.

Sponsors & Supporters
