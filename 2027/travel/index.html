Travel & Visa

AsiaJCIS 2027

Getting There

The conference is held at Juntendo University, Urayasu-Hinode Campus, on the Tokyo Bay waterfront.

  • From Haneda Airport (HND): about 40 minutes (recommended for international arrivals).
  • From Narita Airport (NRT): about 60 minutes.
  • From Tokyo Station: about 20 minutes by train to Shin-Urayasu Station, then about 10 minutes by bus.
  • A shuttle bus for participants is being planned.

See the Venue page for the map and detailed access.

Visa Information

Participants who require a visa to enter Japan are responsible for arranging their own visa. Please check the requirements with the Japanese embassy or consulate in your country or region well in advance, as processing can take several weeks.

Whether a visa is granted is decided solely by the Japanese immigration authorities and the relevant embassy or consulate. The organizers have no influence over visa decisions and cannot guarantee that a visa will be issued.

Letter of Invitation

Upon request, the organizers can provide a letter of invitation (or a letter of participation) to support a visa application. Please note:

  • Letters are issued only to authors of accepted papers who have completed registration and to registered participants.
  • A letter only confirms the person's association with the conference. It does not imply any financial responsibility on the part of the organizers, and does not guarantee that a visa will be approved.
  • Requests should be made after registration. Please allow sufficient time before your intended travel date.

To request a letter, please see the Contact page.
