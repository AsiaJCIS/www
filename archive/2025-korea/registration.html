Notice
( for Authors of Accepted Papers )

  • For each accepted paper, at least one of the authors is required to register as a General Participant (Offline Attendance).
    • - This applies regardless of whether the registering author is a student or not.
  • In cases where both a student author and a non-student author attend the conference, the student author may register as a Student (Offline Attendance), while the non-student author is required to register as a General Participant (Offline Attendance).
