Step 1 — Choose Your Arrival Airport

Participants may fly into either of the two major airports serving the Guangdong region:

SZX
Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport

Bao'an District, Shenzhen

Guangdong Province, China

CAN
Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport

No. 888 Jichang Avenue East, Huadu District

Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, China

From either airport, take a train to Changping Station (常平站), then take a taxi to Guangdong University of Science and Technology, Songshan Lake Campus.

* Take a taxi from the airport to any of the following stations:

Guangzhou East Station (广州东站) Guangzhou Xintang Station (广州新塘站) Guangzhou Baiyun Station (广州白云站)
Travel Tips
  • Book train tickets in advance via 12306.cn (English) to secure your preferred schedule.
  • At Changping Station (常平站), take a taxi directly to 广东科技学院松山湖校区.
  • For maps and navigation within China, use Gaode Maps (高德地图) — Google Maps has limited functionality in mainland China.
  • Book accommodation via Trip.com. The Lavande Hotel is recommended (see sidebar).
